# Prajavani (PV) Short Forms

_Date: 12 Oct 2026 · Companion to Home-Top-Collection-Requirements.md, section 5_

PV shows the short form of every time and read time label on desktop and mobile. This list gives each short form with its English meaning beside it. Forms marked * are proposed and are not in the prototype; the PV desk confirms the Kannada wording.

## 1. Abbreviations

| Short form | Full Kannada word | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ನಿ. | ನಿಮಿಷ / ನಿಮಿಷಗಳ | minute / minutes |
| ಗಂ. | ಗಂಟೆ / ಗಂಟೆಗಳ | hour / hours |
| ದಿ. * | ದಿನ / ದಿನಗಳ | day / days |
| ಹಿಂದೆ | ಹಿಂದೆ | ago |
| ಓದು | ಓದು | read |

## 2. Time labels

Numbers are shown as digits (0 to 9). N stands for the number.

| Case | PV short form | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under 1 minute | ಈಗಷ್ಟೇ * | Just now |
| 1 minute | 1 ನಿ. ಹಿಂದೆ * | 1 minute ago |
| 2 to 59 minutes | N ನಿ. ಹಿಂದೆ, for example 30 ನಿ. ಹಿಂದೆ | N minutes ago, for example 30 minutes ago |
| 1 hour | 1 ಗಂ. ಹಿಂದೆ | 1 hour ago |
| 2 to 23 hours | N ಗಂ. ಹಿಂದೆ, for example 4 ಗಂ. ಹಿಂದೆ | N hours ago, for example 4 hours ago |
| 1 day (24 to 47 hours) | 1 ದಿ. ಹಿಂದೆ * | 1 day ago |
| 2 to 6 days | N ದಿ. ಹಿಂದೆ, for example 2 ದಿ. ಹಿಂದೆ * | N days ago, for example 2 days ago |
| 7 days or more | Publish date in Kannada, format to be confirmed * | Publish date, for example 12 Oct 2026 |

## 3. Read time labels

Read time is the number of minutes from the CMS API, shown as it comes.

| Case | PV short form | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 minute | 1 ನಿ. ಓದು * | 1 min read |
| 2 minutes or more | N ನಿ. ಓದು, for example 6 ನಿ. ಓದು | N min read, for example 6 min read |
